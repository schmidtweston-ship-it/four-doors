About

Four Doors is a tool. A mixture. The person chooses.

There is a link for someone who has never set up: they land on the doors. Share go.html.

There is a link for when they cannot choose: they land on a hold with no doors. Share hold.html.

Door 1 meets you where you are, then walks. That match-then-shift idea comes from Ira Altshuler's music work in the 1940s.

Door 2 goes to the destination first. Music therapists call that compensation (Wigram and others).

Door 3 locks to a body tempo and moves it. People call that kind of locking entrainment.

Door 4 is a named partner and a four-count. Presence, and a beat the body can hold when words fail.

The mixture is the point. Evidence for any one of these, used this way, is mixed and small. This is not a treatment. It is not medical advice. It does not diagnose. You can stop. You can pick another door.

Color is a layer on every door, not a fifth door. Sound is made in the browser unless you add your own tracks.
